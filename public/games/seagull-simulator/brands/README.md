# Seagull Simulator brand signage

These assets are locally bundled environmental shop signage for Seagull Simulator: Dublin. They are used to make the cartoon Dublin streetscape more recognisable.

Third-party brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Their appearance in the game is contextual background signage only and does not imply sponsorship, partnership or endorsement by Duck & Bear.

The game does not hotlink the brand websites at runtime.

- SuperValu — https://supervalu.ie/ — current public logo asset: `/images/supervalu.svg`

- Boots Ireland — https://www.boots.ie/ — current blue script/oval identity
- Brown Thomas — https://www.brownthomas.com/ — current black wordmark/storefront identity
- Bewley's — https://bewleys.com/ — current public logo asset: `/cdn/shop/files/bew-logo.png`

The local SVGs are sized/contained for the game's cartoon fascia panels so the storefront remains legible on a phone screen.
