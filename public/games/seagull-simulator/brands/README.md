# Seagull Simulator brand signage

These assets are locally bundled environmental shop signage for Seagull Simulator: Dublin. They are used to make the cartoon Dublin streetscape more recognisable.

Third-party brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners. Their appearance in the game is contextual background signage only and does not imply sponsorship, partnership or endorsement by Duck & Bear.

The game does not hotlink the brand websites at runtime.

## Public identity references

- Centra — https://centra.ie/ — current public logo asset: `/images/centra_logo_next_gen_transparent.png`
- SuperValu — https://supervalu.ie/ — current public logo asset: `/images/supervalu.svg`
- SPAR Ireland — https://www.spar.ie/ — current public logo asset: `/wp-content/themes/spar.ie/images/logo.svg`
- Tesco Ireland — https://www.tesco.ie/ — current Tesco wordmark identity; local multicolour SVG derived from the public brand mark.
- McDonald's Ireland — https://www.mcdonalds.com/ie/en-ie.html — current public golden-arches logo asset under `/content/dam/sites/ie/nfl/logo/`
- Boots Ireland — https://www.boots.ie/ — current blue script/oval identity
- Brown Thomas — https://www.brownthomas.com/ — current black wordmark/storefront identity
- Bewley's — https://bewleys.com/ — current public logo asset: `/cdn/shop/files/bew-logo.png`

The local SVGs are sized/contained for the game's cartoon fascia panels so the storefront remains legible on a phone screen.

- Yeeros — https://www.yeeros.ie/ — current Dublin storefront wordmark reference and the project-supplied logo reference.
- Disney — current Disney wordmark reference; local vector wordmark bundled for environmental signage.
- LEGO — official current LEGO logo reference: https://assets.lego.com/logos/v8.5.0/brand-lego.svg

Build 1.0.1 removes the game use of the previous Insomnia sign and adds Tesco, Yeeros, Disney and LEGO. The route is deliberately compressed and stylised; environmental placement does not state a precise real-world tenancy.
